Why the turn ended七种结束的理由

node scripts/sync.mjs

Every notifier decides “done” from agent/status → idle, which is true both when a turn finishes and when you press stop. The reason was always in the session log — DSH writes it there itself. 每个通知插件都靠 agent/status → idle 判断「完成了」， 可这个信号在正常结束和你按下停止时完全相同。原因其实一直都在会话日志里 —— DSH 自己写的。
